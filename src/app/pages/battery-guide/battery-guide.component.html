Battery Brand Tier List

LiFePO4 batteries ranked by cost per Wh — lower ₱/Wh = better value. Prices include 10% markup.

How to Read This Guide

S Tier = Best value (lowest ₱/Wh)  |  A Tier = Good value  |  B Tier = Mid-range  |  C Tier = Premium price.

All prices are estimated retail in Philippine Peso (PHP). ₱/Wh = total battery price divided by capacity in watt-hours. Compatible with Solis, Deye, and LuxPower hybrid inverters via CAN/RS485.
